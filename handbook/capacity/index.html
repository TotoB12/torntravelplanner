Maximize how many items you can bring back from each trip

Base Carrying Capacity

5

Base Capacity

Without any upgrades, you can carry 5 items from abroad

This can be significantly increased through various upgrades, job perks, faction specials, and special events. With full optimization, you can carry 44 items per trip (88 during Tourism Day)!

Airstrip & Pilot

15 base capacity

A property upgrade for a Private Island that sets your base capacity to 15, 30% faster flights, and free travel (no flight costs). Requires a Private Island with the Airstrip upgrade and hiring a Pilot staff member.

For more info and calculations, go to the airstrip section in Upgrades & Items

Excursion Faction Special

+1 to +10 items

Each upgrade level of the Excursion faction special adds +1 carrying capacity, up to a maximum of +10. Also reduces travel fees, increases hunting income, reduces rehab costs, and increases Cayman interest.

Business Class Ticket (BCT)

15 base capacity

Using a BCT sets your base capacity to 15 items (replaces the default 5) and provides 70% faster flights. This is a consumable item used once per round trip.

Tourism Day

×2 capacity

Tourism Day is a special event that occurs periodically in Torn, generally between 26 and 28 September. During Tourism Day events, your total carrying capacity is doubled! This is the best time to make high-volume import runs.

"Smuggling For Beginners" Book

+10 items

Reading this book increases your carrying capacity by 10 items for 31 days.

Wind Lines Travel (WLT) Shares

15 base capacity

Owning 9 million WLT stock shares grants access to a private jet. This increases your base capacity to 15 capacity and provides 50% faster flights.
