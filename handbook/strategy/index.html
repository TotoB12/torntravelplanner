Strategy & Tips

Maximize your travel profits and efficiency with these proven strategies

Tourism Day

Save your big hauls for Tourism Day when capacity is doubled! This is a special event that occurs periodically in Torn, generally between 26 and 28 September. Plan your books and upgrades ahead of time.

Utilizing Special Features

Offshore Banking

Way to protect money from muggers. You do get passive profit from offshore banking deposits, but the ROI is not great. You are generally better off investing this cash in the City Bank early on.

Rehab Centre

Visit Switzerland after drug use to reset addiction and happiness. Excursion bonus reduces costs.

Hunting

South Africa hunting can become profitable later on. Early on, hunting might not be the best use of your energy. However, if your natural energy gets full during travel anyway, you can hunt without "wasting" energy.

Fortune Teller

Check level progress in China, generally not worth traveling for.

Risk Management

Grace Period Usage

Use the 15-second landing grace period wisely. Log in before landing abroad to quickly buy and fly back before you get mugged. And when landing back in Torn be ready to ghost trade or quickly deposit to your faction vault.

Status Visibility

Your traveling status is visible to others. Some jobs (like theatre) obscure your exact location. This can be helpful during wars or if you have enemies watching your movements.

General Tips

•

Monitor restock times: Popular items sell out quickly. Track when shops restock to get the best inventory.

•

Join high-Excursion factions: The faction bonus is huge. Even if you're a casual player, this significantly boosts profits.

•

Plan for Tourism Day: Mark it on your calendar. Stack all upgrades and plan major hauls for this event.

•

Consider job timing: Work at Lingerie Store for 10★ before switching to another job. The free BCT travel is invaluable.

•

Check market prices: Item values fluctuate. Before committing to a destination, verify current market prices.
