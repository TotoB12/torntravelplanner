Upgrades & Items

Items and upgrades that enhance your travel capabilities

Airstrip & Pilot

The Airstrip is a property upgrade that provides significant travel benefits when combined with a hired Pilot.

Must have!

The Airstrip is one of the most important investments you can make when starting out with traveling. Buying one is too expensive, but renting one is the best thing you can save up for early on.

Ways to obtain (ordered by cost)

  1. Marry someone who owns a private island with an airstrip (Free, unless otherwise negotiated with spouse).
  2. Rent a private island (400K - 1M per day, depending on modifications). The increased profits make this worthwhile, and you also get better happiness for improved training.
  3. Buy a private island (500M+). This is more end-game content. Generally, marrying or renting is more cost-effective, allowing you to invest your money elsewhere.

Suitcases

Business Class Ticket (BCT)

Benefits

  • •Make use of Business Class travel method
    • •70% faster travel time
    • •Base capacity of 15 items

How to Obtain

  • • Buying from Item Market (Current price: ~$5.5M)
  • • Lingerie Store 10★ (permanent free BCT ticket)
  • • City finds (random)
  • • Casino wheel prizes
  • • Special events

Usage Rules

BCTs are consumed when you start a trip. One ticket covers the round trip (both ways). 10★ Lingerie Store employees get free BCT travel without consuming tickets.

Travel-Related Books

"Mailing Yourself Abroad"

-25% travel time

Reading this book reduces your travel time by 25% for 31 days. This stacks with other speed bonuses.

"Smuggling For Beginners"

+10 capacity

Reading this book increases your carrying capacity by 10 items for 31 days.

Books can be obtained through missions, subscriber rewards, or rarely found in the city.
