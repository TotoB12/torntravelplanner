What Can You Do While Traveling?

Profit Trading

Import items from abroad and sell them on the market for significant profits, especially during Tourism Day events.

Offshore Banking

Deposit money in the Cayman Islands bank to earn monthly interest, protected from mugging while you're online.

Rehab

Visit the Swiss Rehab Centre to reduce drug addiction levels and refill your happiness bar.

Hunting

Use energy to hunt wild animals in South Africa for cash rewards, experience points, and special honors.

Fortune Telling

Pay the Fortune Teller in China to view your exact progress toward your next level.

Basic Travel Mechanics

  • •Unlock at Level 15: Travel becomes available once you reach level 15 in Torn.
  • •Cannot Be Attacked: You cannot be attacked while in transit.
  • •15-Second Grace Period: When you land back in Torn or abroad, you have a 15-second window before others can attack you.
  • •Base Capacity: You can carry 5 items by default, but this can be significantly increased with upgrades.
  • •Varied Flight Times: Each destination has different travel times and costs. The actual flight time varies by ±3% from the base time.
