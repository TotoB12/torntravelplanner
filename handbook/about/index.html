About This Handbook

This comprehensive travel handbook is designed to help Torn players understand and master the travel system. Whether you're a new player just unlocking travel at level 15 or a veteran looking to optimize your profits, this handbook covers everything you need to know.

The handbook is part of the Torn Travel Planner project, a community tool designed to help players plan and execute profitable travel runs.
