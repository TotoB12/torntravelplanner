What You'll Find at Each Destination

Every destination in Torn has three types of shops:

General Store

The main items to go after are flowers and plushies. Other miscellaneous items can be used for crimes and other activities, and may also be profitable.

Black Market

Drugs, contraband, and rare artifacts. These items can be profitable, but are generally more expensive and frequently out of stock. However, they can yield potentially high profits.

Arms Dealer

Combat items exclusive to each country. Generally not profitable to buy and resell. Some temporaries are worth buying (e.g. Tear Gas, Smoke Grenade), but they are often out of stock and not a reliable profit source.
