Tips for Japan

  • •Cherry Blossom: Solid profitable item with slow decay time, making it a solid profitable destination.
  • •Xanax: Good place to buy Xanax, generally good profit when available. One of the best places to buy Xanax, along with South Africa.
  • •Other items: Plenty other good profitable OC-related items, like Glow Stick, Bonded Latex or Whale Meat. Check the prices and profit before buying when they are in stock.
  • •Long flight: From Japan and onward, even with an Airstrip the flight is quite long and could waste some of your natural energy regen. Be aware and plan accordingly.
