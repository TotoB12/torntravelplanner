Companies & Faction Perks

Job specials and faction upgrades that enhance travel

About Companies

Companies in Torn are player-owned businesses that provide various perks to employees based on their star level. Company star levels range from 1 to 10 stars and depend on how well the company performs. Higher star levels unlock more powerful perks that can significantly enhance your travel experience.

Each company has different perks available at different star levels. The star level shown next to each perk indicates the minimum star level required for that company to unlock that particular benefit.

Excursion Faction Special

Faction Upgrade

The Excursion special is a faction upgrade that leaders can purchase using respect points. Each upgrade level provides increasingly powerful travel bonuses.

Capacity

+1 item per level (max +10)

Travel Cost

Reduced flight fees per level

Hunting Income

Increased hunting rewards in South Africa

Rehab Cost

Cheaper rehab in Switzerland

Cayman Interest

Increased offshore bank interest

Tip: When looking for a faction, check their Excursion level! Joining a faction with high Excursion upgrades immediately grants you all the benefits.
