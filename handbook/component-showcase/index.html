  • PageHeader - Page title with gradient background

  • SectionHeader - Section title with optional icon and action
  • InfoCard - Callout cards with optional icon

  • SubCard - Nested content cards (NEW)
  • StatDisplay - Large number displays
  • Badge - Small colored labels
  • NumberedList - Numbered steps
  • IconList - Lists with icon bullets
  • NavigationLinks - Previous/Next links

11. Callout Component (NEW)

Props: colorTheme, icon?, title?, children, variant?

Usage Rules

BCTs are consumed when you start a trip. One ticket covers the round trip (both ways). 10★ Lingerie Store employees get free BCT travel without consuming tickets.

Important: Suitcases do NOT stack! Only the largest suitcase you own provides its bonus.

Detective Agency Delay: If someone hires a Detective to locate you, your return flight may be delayed.
