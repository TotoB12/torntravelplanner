Getting started

Understanding flight times, costs, and how to stay safe while traveling

Book your first flight!

  1. 1Reach level 15 to unlock the Travel Agency.
  2. 2Navigate to the Travel Agency in Torn City.
  3. 3Select your ticket type (Standard, Airstrip, Private, or Business).
  4. 4Select your destination.
  5. 5Pay the flight cost and begin your journey.
  6. 6Once arrived, buy the items with the most profit. When done, fly back to Torn City.
  7. 7Sell the items for profit back in Torn ($$$)

For reference, you are able to earn up to $5M+ profit per day eventually...

Travel Upgrades

There are many ways to improve profits, mainly by increasing carrying capacity and reducing flight times. These are a couple of those to give an idea, other pages delve deeper into the details.

Note: Some of these modifiers are quite expensive and may only be practical for end-game use. Plan your investments accordingly.

Airstrip & Pilot

Must have

Property upgrade that provides 30% faster flights, free travel and increases base capacity to 15.

Suitcases

Must have

Suitcases, bought from Hawaii, give a +2, +3 or +4 capacity bonus for small, medium, or large suitcases. Cost from 1M to 10M each.

Business Class Ticket (BCT)

One-time use consumable ticket for a single return trip. Costs around 5-6M.

  • 70% faster flights
  • 15 base capacity

"Mailing Yourself Abroad" Book

Reading this book reduces all travel times by 25% for 31 days. Stacks with other bonuses.

Books are obtained through missions or subscriber rewards.

Wind Lines Travel (WLT) Shares

Owning 9 million WLT stock shares grants a private jet with 50% faster flights and provides immunity agains flight delay.

Expensive: At the time of writing this the stocks will cost around 7 billion. For most people, the 20% extra travel speed is not worth the cost.

Safety Rules

Cannot Be Attacked In Transit

While your plane is in the air, you are completely immune to attacks. Your status shows as "Traveling."

15-Second Grace Period

When you land abroad or back in Torn, you have 15 seconds of attack immunity before other players can attack you.

"Abroad" Status

While in another country, your profile shows "Abroad." Others can see you're traveling but cannot attack you directly, unless they are in the same country.

"Hiding Out" Status

Certain job specials or situations can show "Hiding out" instead, concealing your exact location abroad.

Hospital & Revive Considerations

If you're hospitalized abroad, you cannot fly home while in the hospital. Make use of the 15-second grace period! Note: Being in a 5★ Logistics Management company allows you to travel while in the hospital abroad.

Cannot Travel While Being Attacked

If someone initiates an attack on you, you cannot start a new flight until the attack is finished.
